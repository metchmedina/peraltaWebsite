Frequently Asked Questions

BIM (Building Information Modeling) is a digital process that involves creating and managing detailed 3D models of buildings. It integrates architectural, structural, and MEP data to support planning, design, construction, and operation throughout a project’s lifecycle.

BIM improves design accuracy, reduces errors, saves time and cost, enhances coordination among teams, and allows better visualization before construction begins. It also supports efficient project planning and long-term asset management.

BIM enables all project stakeholders—architects, engineers, contractors, and clients—to work on a shared digital model. This real-time collaboration reduces misunderstandings, improves communication, and ensures everyone works with updated information.

Clash detection is the process of identifying conflicts between different building systems (such as structural, electrical, and plumbing elements) within a BIM model before construction. This helps prevent costly rework and delays on site.
